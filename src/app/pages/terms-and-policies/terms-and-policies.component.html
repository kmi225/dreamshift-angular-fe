Refund Policy

Thank you for choosing DreamShift, a premium Australian career support service backed by a 60-day money-back guarantee. We are committed to helping you land interviews and move your career forward.

60-Day Guarantee

If you do not land at least one job interview within 60 days of receiving your finalized documents, you may request one of the following:

  • A 50% refund of your service fee, or
  • A complete rewrite of all documents (CV, Cover Letter and LinkedIn) at no additional cost

The 60-day guarantee period begins from the date your finalized documents are delivered to you.

Conditions for the Guarantee

To qualify for the 60-day guarantee, all of the following must apply:

  1. You must have actively applied to relevant job roles using the documents we provided during the 60-day period
  2. The roles applied for must be relevant to your field, experience level and visa eligibility
  3. Your claim must be submitted within 75 days of receiving your finalized documents

Non-Refundable Services

The following are not covered by the refund policy:

  • Fully completed and approved services where the 75-day claim window has passed
  • Revisions requested beyond the original agreed scope of work
  • Express or expedited delivery services
  • Services where the client did not actively apply to relevant roles during the 60-day period

Refund Process

  1. Contact us at {{ adminEmail }} to submit your claim
  2. Our team will review and respond within 3–5 business days
  3. If approved, refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 4–7 business days
